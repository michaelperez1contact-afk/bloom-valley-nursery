Plant Care Guide

This page gives customers simple plant care tips and helpful gardening information.

Watering Tips

Check the soil before watering. Many plants only need water when the top layer of soil feels dry.

Sunlight Tips

Different plants need different amounts of sunlight. Indoor plants usually do best with bright, indirect light.

Soil and Fertilizer

Healthy soil helps plants grow stronger. Customers can choose soil and fertilizer based on the type of plant they are growing.

Community Events

Bloom Valley Nursery hosts local gardening events and seasonal workshops for customers who want to learn more about plant care.
